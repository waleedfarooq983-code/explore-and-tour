A Land of Wonders

Everything you need to know about the Islamic Republic of Pakistan

Pakistan is a country of extraordinary contrasts — from the world's highest mountain ranges in the north to the sun-drenched beaches of the Arabian Sea in the south. Home to over 240 million people speaking more than 70 languages, Pakistan is a mosaic of cultures, landscapes, and traditions.

Whether you're trekking to K2, exploring the ancient ruins of Mohenjo-Daro, savoring Lahore's legendary food, or boating on Attabad Lake — Pakistan promises an adventure of a lifetime.

Four Provinces, One Country

Each region offers a unique blend of landscapes and traditions

North

Gilgit-Baltistan

Home to the Karakoram, Himalayas, and Hindu Kush ranges — including K2 and some of the world's largest glaciers.

Heart

Punjab

The cultural heart of Pakistan — Lahore's Mughal architecture, food streets, and warm hospitality define this province.

South

Sindh

Ancient Indus Valley civilization, Karachi's modern energy, and the ruins of Mohenjo-Daro — a land of history and coast.

West

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat's green valleys, Kalash culture, and the gateway to the Khyber Pass — Pakistan's frontier of beauty.

A Glimpse Through Time

From the Indus Valley to modern Pakistan

3000 BCE

Indus Valley Civilization

One of the world's oldest civilizations flourishes at Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa, with advanced urban planning, drainage systems, and trade networks.

326 BCE

Alexander the Great

Alexander's armies cross into the region, leaving a lasting cultural impact on Gandhara.

1526 – 1857

Mughal Empire

The Mughals build their greatest monuments — Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, Shalimar Gardens, and the Taj Mahal in neighbouring India.

1858 – 1947

British Raj

Under British rule, railways, universities, and modern infrastructure reshape the subcontinent.

14 August 1947

Independence

Pakistan gains independence as a homeland for the Muslims of South Asia. Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah becomes the first Governor-General.

Today

Modern Pakistan

A vibrant, nuclear-armed nation of 240+ million people — a key player in South Asia with rapidly growing tourism.
